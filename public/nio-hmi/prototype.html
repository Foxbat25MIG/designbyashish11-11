Owner’s manual
menu_bookGetting startedexpand_more

Unlock with your phone key or card, sit in, and press the brake: the car is ready to drive. Choose D or R on the gear selector. To switch off, select P and leave the car; it locks itself as you walk away.

ev_stationCharging and Power Swapexpand_more

Open the charge port from Quick controls or press its flap. Plug in at any AC or DC charger; the screen shows speed and time left. For a Power Swap, choose a station in Navigation and the car drives itself into the bay.

shieldDriver assistsexpand_more

Assists help with speed, steering and lane changes, but you stay responsible. Keep your hands on the wheel and eyes on the road. Turn individual assists on or off in Car › Driving.
