Nio

Overview

A modular, intelligent centre console for the NIO ET9, focused on clarity, comfort and seamless interaction across driving, navigation, media and lifestyle, built as a live, clickable prototype with a real-time 3D car.

About the project

Create a calm, intuitive and visually balanced interface that delivers essential vehicle and lifestyle information with minimal distraction, while reflecting NIO’s premium, tech-forward, human-centric design philosophy.

The project studies NIO’s design language and turns it into a working centre screen for the ET9 flagship sedan: one design system, light and dusk themes, and a real-time 3D model of the car that responds to every control — doors, windows, sunshades, lights, ride height and charging.

The story follows Emma, a designer in Luxembourg, through one afternoon: unlocking the car, driving to the Mudam, reserving parking and charging.

Design approach

Driving comes first. Everything the driver needs at speed sits in the status bar and the bottom dock; everything else lives one tap away in calm, card-based pages. Controls are large touch targets with clear states, and every panel opens and closes with the same easing, so the screen always feels like one system.

Key features

Each part of the dashboard answers one moment in Emma’s afternoon.

01 · LOCK AND HOME

Unlock to a living scene

The lock screen and Home place the ET9 in animated rolling hills with wind in the grass. Drag to turn the car; widgets fold into pills.

02 · QUICK CONTROLS

Control the car, see it happen

Doors, frunk, boot, charge port, windows, curved sunshades, mirrors, lights and hazards, all animated on the 3D model from above.

03 · SKYRIDE

Five drive modes, five moods

Comfort, Eco, Sport, Snow and Custom each get their own landscape, light and ride height, with real night headlights.

04 · NAVIGATION

Safety in context

Turn-by-turn on a real Luxembourg map; when a cyclist approaches, the blind-spot camera and a red edge warning appear.

05 · CHARGING

Energy you can see

Plug in: the flap opens, the cable glides in and a glowing battery fills, with live %, range, kW and time to full.

06 · COMFORT

Four-zone climate and seats

Per-seat temperature, aimable vents with sweep, seat heating and massage, and the rear executive table.

Design system

A soft lavender palette for day and a violet dusk for night, built on Red Hat Display and Red Hat Text with Material Symbols. Colour is used for state, never for decoration: green for charging, red for warnings, amber for hazards.

Built for the web

The whole dashboard runs in the browser. One shared WebGL renderer draws every 3D view, so it works on phones and tablets too. The car’s body was refined and baked ahead of time for smooth reflections, and the lock screen and Home load first so you can unlock in a few seconds.

This is a personal design study, not an official NIO product. I studied NIO’s design system and created my own recreation. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Vehicle figures follow published NIO ET9 specifications where available; some values are adjusted for the design study.
